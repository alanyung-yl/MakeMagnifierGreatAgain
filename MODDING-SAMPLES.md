# Modding Samples

[Back to the README](README.md)

Examples of MMGA sight assemblies and their in-game views. The screenshots were captured on SPT 4.1.6. They illustrate attachment placement and sight pictures.

## M4A1: SkeetIR, EXPS3, and G33

SkeetIR sits ahead of an EOTech EXPS3 and an FDE G33 on a Unity FAST FTC mount. The build view shows the separate optics and risers arranged along the rail.

![M4A1 build with SkeetIR, EXPS3, Unity FAST risers, and an FDE G33 FTC assembly](<screenshots/Screenshot 2026-10-25 175746.png>)

## M4A1: Aimpoint CompM5 and G33

An Aimpoint CompM5 on a Unity FAST Micro mount is paired with a black G33 FTC assembly. This example shows a magnifier setup without a thermal clip-on.

![M4A1 build with an Aimpoint CompM5 on a FAST Micro mount and a black G33 FTC assembly](<screenshots/Screenshot 2026-10-25 181145.png>)

## Thermal sight pictures

### 1× view

The thermal image is visible through the holographic sight, with the G33 folded aside. The sight supplies the aiming reticle.

![One-power thermal sight picture through an EOTech holographic sight with the G33 folded aside](<screenshots/Screenshot 2026-10-25 182307.png>)

### 3× view through the G33

With the G33 inline, the magnifier displays the thermal image and the attached sight's reticle.

![Three-power thermal sight picture through the G33 showing the holographic reticle](<screenshots/Screenshot 2026-10-25 182327.png>)

### Magnified scope view

The attached scope retains its own reticle over the thermal image.

![Thermal sight picture through a magnified scope with its native crosshair](<screenshots/Screenshot 2026-10-25 182424.png>)

Placement and clipping can vary with the chosen optics and mounts. WeaponCustomizer can help position compatible assemblies. See the README's [compatibility and known issues](README.md#compatibility-and-known-issues) before recreating a setup.
